Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

What Kairoos reads from TikTok

When a creator connects a TikTok account, Kairoos receives the account's display name, avatar, username and open ID, and the account's posting options (such as the privacy levels it can use). It also receives an access token and a refresh token, which Kairoos uses to upload and publish videos on the creator's behalf.

What Kairoos does with it

This data is used only to show which account is connected and to upload the videos the creator chooses to post. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Kairoos runs on the developer's own computer. The tokens and account details are stored in its local database, and the videos are stored in its local files. Videos are deleted after they are published or by routine cleanup. Nothing is stored on a third-party server by Kairoos.

Revoking access and deleting data

The creator can disconnect the account inside Kairoos, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. To ask for any other stored data to be deleted, write to novacatedral14@gmail.com.
