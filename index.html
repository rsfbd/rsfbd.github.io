Kairoos

Kairoos is a tool for video creators. It turns long videos into short vertical clips, lets the creator edit them, and publishes them to the creator's own social accounts.

For TikTok, Kairoos uses Login Kit to connect the creator's own account and the Content Posting API to upload the clips the creator chose to post. Nothing is posted without the creator reviewing the video and confirming it.

Kairoos is currently used by its developer, on the developer's own computer.
